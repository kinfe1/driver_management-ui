# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are admin and operations staff who manage driver rosters, compliance filings, vehicle assignments, and document status across a transport fleet. Secondary users are drivers who need a simple, reliable view of assigned vehicles, upcoming expiries, and current status so they can stay on the road without compliance gaps.

## Product Purpose

Provide a lightweight fleet operations workspace that centralizes driver management, document compliance, and vehicle assignment workflows in one responsive browser app. Success is measured by faster admin decisions, fewer expired credentials, and drivers who trust the system for assignment clarity.

## Positioning

A practical, role-aware management dashboard that keeps admin operators in control of driver compliance while giving drivers a clear, focused home for assignment status.

## Brand Personality

Confident, efficient, and dependable.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboard chrome, splashy marketing pages, and overly decorative data visuals. This should not feel like a sales landing page, a consumer mobile app, or a bloated enterprise suite.

## Design Principles

- Make key actions visible and easy to reach for each role.
- Use calm structure and consistent spacing to reduce operational friction.
- Surface compliance status clearly so problems are obvious at a glance.
- Keep admin and driver experiences distinct but aligned in tone.
- Favor responsive layout and clean information hierarchy over decorative styling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Design for readable contrast, clear focus order, and reduced-motion preference. Ensure form fields, tables, and navigation are easy to scan for users on desktop and smaller screens.
